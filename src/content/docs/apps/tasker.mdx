## How a rule works

A rule combines a trigger, optional conditions, and one or more actions. For example, a
charging-state condition can show a notification. Advanced rules can branch, wait, open an
app, speak a message, or change a supported vehicle setting.

You can use Tasker without MG4 Control. Control is needed only when a rule applies a profile
that was saved in Control.

## What to expect

Test a rule while parked before enabling it. Vehicle changes follow the same standstill
safety checks as MG4 Control, and the history screen shows what ran or why it was refused.

Start with [Write your first rule](/MG4Suite/tutorial/first-rule/). Test it while parked,
inspect its execution history, and only then enable it for normal use.
