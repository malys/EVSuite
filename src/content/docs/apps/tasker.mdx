MG4 Tasker runs local rules on the head unit: conditions trigger actions, branches, delays,
notifications, app launches, or supported vehicle changes. It does not need MG4 Control
unless a rule applies a profile saved there.

## Rule model

A rule has a trigger and one or more conditions and actions. The shared MG4Hardware
catalogue supplies vehicle-aware entries and filters them by firmware. Expert mode reveals
advanced composition without making the default editor busier.

Start with [Write your first rule](/MG4Suite/tutorial/first-rule/). Test it while parked,
inspect its execution history, and only then enable it for normal use.
