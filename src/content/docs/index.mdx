MG4Suite is not one monolithic app. It is a family of small, independently installable
projects with one safety policy, one visual language, and one shared vehicle-access layer.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Control" icon="setting">
    Change supported vehicle settings and save reusable driving profiles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tasker" icon="seti:settings">
    Build local rules from vehicle conditions and carefully gated actions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ABRP telemetry" icon="rocket">
    Send read-only vehicle telemetry to A Better Routeplanner.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Launchers" icon="menu">
    Replace the home screen or open a chosen app with a bottom-edge swipe.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Built for one environment

The suite targets the MG4 infotainment system: Android Automotive OS 9 on MT2712, in a
landscape 1920×720 application area. Vehicle-writing apps refuse road-behaviour changes
unless speed is confirmed at 0 km/h. The launchers do not access the vehicle at all.

> MG4Suite is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with SAIC Motor or
> MG Motor. Installing third-party software on a vehicle is at your own risk.
