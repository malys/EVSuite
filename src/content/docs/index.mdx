MG4Suite is a collection of independent apps. Install only the ones that solve a problem
for you; you do not need the complete suite.

## Start in three steps

1. [Choose the apps that match your needs](/MG4Suite/start/overview/).
2. [Read the safety information](/MG4Suite/start/safety/).
3. [Open Android Settings](/MG4Suite/tutorial/settings-access/) and follow the installation guide.

> MG4Suite is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with SAIC Motor or
> MG Motor. Installing third-party software on a vehicle is at your own risk.
